About Scoops Ice Cream

Handcrafted artisan ice cream churned daily with fresh, locally sourced ingredients.

Our Story

Founded in 2026, Scoops was created to bring high-quality, authentic gelato and ice cream to our local community. We believe that great ice cream starts with honest ingredients—fresh dairy from local farms, real fruit, and zero artificial preservatives.

🌾 100% Local

We source our dairy and fruit directly from regional farms to support local producers and guarantee freshness.

🍨 Churned Daily

Every single flavor on our menu is small-batch churned right in our kitchen every morning.

🌱 Inclusive Flavors

We believe everyone deserves a treat, offering dedicated vegan, dairy-free, and gluten-free options.
